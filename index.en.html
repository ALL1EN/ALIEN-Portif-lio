    Frequently asked questions

    Questions answered

    How does a project start?

    You reach out on WhatsApp or email and we talk about the goal, the audience and the deadline. Then I send a proposal with scope, stages and pricing.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the scope. A single ad is different from a full campaign or a page with integrations. The timeline is set in the proposal, before we start.

    Which formats do you deliver?

    I deliver for the platforms you use: feed, stories, ads, vertical and horizontal videos and responsive pages. Final files are sent already exported, and editable files are available as agreed.

    How does AI fit into your work?

    I use AI to speed up production, generate variations and automate repetitive tasks. Every output goes through human review before delivery. Creative direction and the final call are mine and yours.

    Shall we talk about your project?

    Tell me the goal, the channel and the result you expect. If it makes sense, we'll continue on WhatsApp.
