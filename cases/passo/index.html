09 · FAQ

Before your
first step.

Can't find yours? Ask on WhatsApp, the front desk replies the same day.

I've never danced. Can I start in an adult class?

You can, and you should. Beginner classes are made exactly for people who have never danced or stopped a long time ago. The teacher adapts the movements and nobody gets left behind.

What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable clothes that let you move and a water bottle. For ballet, tights and ballet slippers help, but they're not required for the trial class.

How do make-up classes work?

Let the front desk know at least 2 hours in advance and make it up in another class of the same level within the same month. The number of make-ups depends on your plan.

Does the show cost extra?

Participation is included in every plan and the costume is lent by the school. Audience tickets are sold separately, at a family-friendly price.

Can parents watch the kids' classes?

So the kids can focus, classes are closed. On the last class of each month the studio opens for families to watch.

Is there a minimum commitment or a cancellation fee?

Not on the monthly plan. Just let us know by the 20th so you aren't charged for the following month. The six-month plan has a discount and its own rules, explained at enrollment.
