The project · overview

Privacy that feels simple.

The brand had to convey security without the heavy mood of defense software. The answer was a shield with a clean cut in the middle, a single electric blue and a constant dark background, where light only appears where the information matters.

Symbol · 01

A shield with a tunnel inside.

The silhouette is a shield: protection, readable at any size. The inner cut repeats the same shape at a smaller scale, like a tunnel the traffic passes through. The diagonal splits the face into light and shadow, giving volume without an outline.

1
Shield

Five sides, a pointed base. It's the shape the eye reads as protection before reading any word.

2
The inner cut

The same shield at a smaller scale, open at the base. It's the tunnel: the path the traffic takes out, protected.

3
The diagonal

Splits the face on the vertical axis and lightens the left side by 22%. Depth without strokes or shadows.

4
16-unit grid

Every point sits on the grid. At 16 px, the icon keeps the inner cut legible in the status bar.

Logotype · 02

Three lockups, one voice.

The name is set in Geist Bold, with spacing tightened by 1%. The symbol matches the cap height and sits half its width away from the text.

Do

  • Minimum clear space equal to the symbol's width around the whole lockup.
  • Minimum symbol height of 20 px on screen, 8 mm in print.
  • On photos, use the flat white version, with the image darkened by 40%.

Don't

  • Change the shield color to anything other than the brand blue or white.
  • Add an outline or drop shadow, or tilt the lockup.
  • Redraw the name in another typeface or separate the symbol from the text horizontally.
Colors · 03

One blue, one night background.

The background almost never changes. Blue appears only on actions: the connect button, the link, the on state. The three alert colors are reserved for system states. Click any color to copy its code.

Typography · 04

Serif for the voice, grotesque for the hand.

PT Serif signs the first two heading levels, where the brand speaks. Geist handles the rest of the interface, where the user acts. Sixteen fixed styles, and nothing outside the list.

Components · 05

Pieces that respond to touch.

16 px corners on blocks and fully rounded buttons. No outlines: what separates an element from the background is a 5.5% white layer and the shadow it casts. Everything here is real, go ahead and play with it.

Screens · 06

Six flows, forty-one screens.

Onboarding, connection, server selection, ad blocking, speed test and account. Every flow starts from the same dark background and uses blue only on the next step.

Applications · 07

The same night, everywhere.

The website takes the identity to the big screen: the planet rises behind the headline, the app appears connected and each section demonstrates a feature instead of describing it.
